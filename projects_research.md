Research work:
- Polyphase Digital Architectures: I investigate polyphase filter and signal processing architectures to enable high-throughput, low-latency digital systems. These structures exploit parallelism across multiple phases to efficiently implement filtering, interpolation, and decimation, particularly for wideband RF and baseband signals.
  
- Massively Parallel Matrix–Vector Multiplication (MVM) Cores: My work involves designing scalable MVM engines optimized for high-speed data flow and minimal latency. These cores support parallel computation of large-scale linear algebra operations, which are critical in applications such as adaptive filtering, beamforming, and AI inference on hardware.
  
- RF and Baseband Signal Processing: I develop end-to-end digital signal processing solutions for RF front-ends and baseband chains, focusing on techniques such as filtering, downconversion, and dynamic range optimization. My designs bridge the gap between RF signal acquisition and efficient digital processing on programmable hardware.
  
- Digital Beamforming and MIMO Systems: I explore designing algorithms and hardware for multi-antenna systems, including MIMO architectures. This includes digital beamforming, spatial multiplexing, and interference mitigation, with an emphasis on hardware-friendly implementations suitable for FPGA and ASIC platforms.

- RF Machine Learning Applications: I explore the integration of machine learning techniques into RF systems to enable spectrum awareness, interference detection, adaptive beamforming, and intelligent signal classification. This research targets the co-design of ML algorithms and hardware accelerators for real-time RF applications.
  
- ASIC-Ready Hardware Accelerators: I explore transforming FPGA prototypes into custom silicon solutions, emphasizing area-, power-, and performance-efficient accelerator designs. This includes mapping parallel signal processing and MVM workloads to ASIC-friendly architectures while preserving flexibility and throughput.
